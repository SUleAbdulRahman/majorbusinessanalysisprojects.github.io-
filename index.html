Pro bono field engagement · Oil palm processing · Eastern Region, Ghana

Agri-Performance Diagnostic for an Oil Palm Processor

A rapid systems diagnostic for a registered oil palm producer and processor. Delivered at no charge in August 2026 as a pilot by AgriValue Advisory Consult, where I am Principal Consultant. The client is not named here.

Scope

A structured interview with the founder and a review of the company's registration profile and business plan. This was a desk-based review, not a site assessment.

Problem

The business had a defined product range, full registration and institutional buyers in view, but could not say what it cost to produce one unit of oil. Prices were set without a cost base.

What I did

  • Ran a structured stakeholder interview with the founder.
  • Reviewed the business plan and company profile line by line, and against each other.
  • Scored six management areas on a five-point maturity scale: cost tracking, production records, governance, market and contracts, compliance and quality, funding readiness.
  • Traced the findings to one root cause: no routine for recording production and converting it into a unit cost.
  • Sequenced recommendations into a 90-day roadmap, led by three that need no capital: a unit costing sheet, a daily production log, and cost-plus-margin pricing.

Status

Diagnostic delivered. Results of implementation are not yet measured.

Tools and techniques: structured stakeholder interview, document and gap analysis, systems maturity scoring, unit cost analysis, root-cause analysis, implementation roadmap.

Training case study · Manufacturing · Quality and root-cause analysis

AutoMotion Manufacturing: Reducing Production Defects

Problem

Vehicles left the line with defects ranging from misaligned panels to electronics faults. Sales fell and customer complaints rose.

Approach

A Fishbone (Ishikawa) analysis traced root causes across People, Methods, Machines, Materials, Measurement and Environment: limited training, outdated machinery, inconsistent supplier materials, infrequent quality checks and unstable factory conditions. Cross-functional teams then acted on each cause: revised inspection routines, workforce development and equipment upgrades.

Case outcome

  • Reduced defect rates
  • 30% rebound in customer satisfaction within months

Tools: gap analysis, Fishbone diagram, requirements elicitation, stakeholder engagement, document and data analysis, MS Excel.

Training case study · Financial services · Process analysis

Chimonica: Loan Application Process (As-Is)

A swimlane map of the current loan process across four parties: customer, loan officers, risk assessment team and bank. The customer visits a branch, completes forms by hand and submits them. Loan officers verify and request further documents when verification fails. The risk team performs further verification, then the loan is denied or approved, with a notification text and disbursement.

The map exposes the manual, branch-bound steps and the rework loop on incomplete submissions, the starting point for an online application.

Tools: swimlane process mapping (Draw.io), as-is analysis.
